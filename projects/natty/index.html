01 / Product

Natty

I was fed up copying workouts out of Notes or another app and pasting them into ChatGPT whenever I wanted useful advice. Natty brings the log, the history and the coaching together in one place.

The approach

Fast for the everyday action, thoughtful around everything else.

Training data is recorded locally first, so a poor connection never gets between a person and their session. Private cloud backup adds continuity across devices, while the coaching layer is kept separate from the core journal.

Project evolution

How my thinking changed

  1. 01 / Friction

    Remove the copy-and-paste loop

    My workouts lived in Notes or another training app, and I was bored of sending them to ChatGPT whenever I wanted an evaluation. Natty began as a way to keep the workout and the feedback together.

  2. 02 / Play

    See if training could feel social

    Once logging the sessions became enjoyable, I started playing with the idea of competing with friends. That experiment became the Natty Score and weekly leaderboards.

  3. 03 / Value

    Keep the useful history close

    The tracking became the part I valued most: progress, personal bests and a useful history of previous sessions, with muscle maps and form videos available when I wanted to revisit an exercise.

  4. 04 / Today

    A personal app with its experiments intact

    By the time I considered the App Store, the market was crowded with similar workout apps. I did not have the heart to strip out the leaderboard or AI, so both live on in what is now mostly a personal app with a few friends playing around. The AI is useful for quick reviews, but I often prefer asking a specific question, which is why I added the option to copy selected workouts into ChatGPT.

Feature history

How Natty has grown

  1. 29 Sep 2026

    Workout copying and background recovery

    Added a way to select and copy readable workout summaries for specific follow-up questions, and made active sessions recover cleanly after the app has been left in the background.

  2. 16 Sep 2026

    Exercise guidance, cardio and better context

    Expanded the exercise library with searchable movements, muscle maps and form videos, added cardio tracking, and included workout notes in coaching feedback.

  3. 13 Sep 2026

    Personal progress and on-demand coaching

    Added progress views and workout controls, then connected workout and weekly reviews to an authenticated, usage-capped Azure AI endpoint.

  4. 12 Sep 2026

    Cloud backup without losing local-first

    Introduced private Supabase backup, password authentication, unfinished-workout autosave and the ability to edit saved sessions.

  5. 03 Sep 2026

    The first complete workout loop

    Shipped the local-first foundation: profile setup, live or manually timed workouts, history, personal bests, Natty Score, and the option to repeat a previous session.
