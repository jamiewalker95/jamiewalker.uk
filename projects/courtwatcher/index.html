02 / Automation

CourtWatcher

Published judgments shouldn't require a daily trawl. CourtWatcher finds new Court of Session opinions, keeps the original material close at hand and delivers a concise digest for review.

The approach

Automate the repetitive work, preserve the route back to the source.

A scheduled service checks for new opinions, downloads and records them, creates structured summaries and sends a Word digest by email. The register and source documents stay durable, so a generated summary never becomes the only record.

Attached notes

What made it dependable

  1. 29 Sep 2026

    Measuring prompt improvements

    Tested seven prompt iterations against a fixed set of 37 opinions, recording misses rather than choosing examples that happened to look convincing.

  2. 15 Sep 2026

    Making state durable

    Used Azure Blob Storage for the register and downloaded opinions so scheduled runs remain repeatable and do not depend on one machine.

  3. 03 Sep 2026

    Treating delivery as part of the product

    Added Microsoft Graph delivery and a readable Word digest, because a good summary has little value if it does not reach the person expecting it.
